# Vivo Northeast Sales & Inventory Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 30 days of Vivo smartphone sales and inventory performance across the Northeast India region.

The analysis focuses on sell-out performance, target achievement, branch-wise trends, model-wise contribution, DOS analysis, stock status, inward supply, and inventory mismatch.

The goal of the project is to identify which models and branches are performing well, where stock risks exist, and what business actions can improve sales and inventory planning.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Data Cleaning
- MIS Reporting
- Business Analysis
- Dashboarding

## Dataset Summary

The dataset contains 1,050 records covering 7 branches and 5 Vivo smartphone models.

Branches covered:

- Guwahati
- Silchar
- Agartala
- Dimapur
- Imphal
- Shillong
- Jorhat

Models tracked:

- Y11
- Y21
- X300FE
- V70FE
- Ultra

## Key Metrics

- Total Sell Out: 24,602 units
- Total Target: 31,916 units
- Overall Achievement: 77.08%
- Total Records: 1,050
- Data Quality Flags: 9 negative closing stock records

## Key Insights

- The overall achievement was 77.08%, meaning the region missed around 23% of its monthly target.
- Y11 and Y21 were the strongest models and together contributed more than half of total sales.
- The 35K+ segment was the weakest performing segment, mainly because of the Ultra model.
- Ultra had high closing stock and high DOS, showing a clear overstock issue.
- Y11 and Y21 had very low DOS, creating a possible stockout risk.
- Silchar was the best-performing branch by achievement percentage.
- Jorhat and Shillong need closer branch-level review.
- 9 records showed negative closing stock and require data validation.

## Business Recommendations

1. Reduce or pause inward supply for Ultra until excess stock is cleared.
2. Prioritize replenishment for Y11 and Y21 to avoid stockouts.
3. Review Jorhat and Shillong branch performance at ground level.
4. Recheck premium segment strategy for Northeast India.
5. Correct negative closing stock records before final reporting.
6. Monitor X300FE and V70FE stock levels to avoid future overstock.
7. Use segment-level performance while setting future sales targets.
